# Reorg - Market Research

Snapshot: **24 Oct 2026**. Figures marked *est.* are third-party estimates; *claimed* are self-reported.

## TL;DR

1. **Single-player office satire is saturated.** 12+ corporate deckbuilders, plus office sims and incrementals.
2. **Both of our seed ideas already exist** (dodge-the-boss, lay-someone-off). Standalone, neither is unique.
3. **Multiplayer office politics is proven but thin.** One real hit (Dale & Dawson), real-time only, decaying player count.
4. **Office workers already play short daily games against colleagues** (LinkedIn Games).
5. **Small teams win with premium + friend-pull**, not free-to-play live ops.

**Our gap:** a strategic, turn-based, async-capable office-politics game for friends and colleagues, with leagues, no install.

## Competitors

### Corporate deckbuilders (crowded)

| Game | Platform | Notes | What it lacks |
|---|---|---|---|
| LADDER | iOS, Android | 600 cards, AI-era tech satire, 4 roles (Engineer to CEO), freemium + daily challenge | Solo only |
| Mr. Magpie's Harmless Card Game | PC, consoles, Game Pass (early 2027) | Corporate horror, Behaviour Interactive publishing, Matt Mercer voice | Solo only |
| Crush the Industry, Ctrl Alt Deal, IT Startup: Tech Fodder, Cards We're Dealt | PC | Office/studio themed deckbuilders | Solo only |
| SEVEIL, Reply All, Bet on Beta, Grounds for Termination, You Are Fired, Chain Corp, dev_hell | PC (upcoming) | More of the same theme | Solo only |

Genre hit rate: roguelike deckbuilders about **5% (11 of 212)**. Slay the Spire 2 dominates (5M+ sold, *claimed*).

### Office sims and incrementals

| Game | Notes |
|---|---|
| Office Life (Dec 2024) | Card-based office politics life sim, 2M words, **89% of ~2K reviews**. Closest solo rival in theme. |
| Office God (Oct 2026), Paperwork (Feb 2026) | Satirical incrementals. Small. |
| Project Manager SIM (2026 demo) | Real-time IT middle-manager sim, satire. Solo. |
| Office Management 101, Office Manager, HR Simulator | Tycoon / building / HR management. Solo. |

### Seed idea 1: dodge the boss, keep work-life balance

UnGrind, NSFW (2016, $2.99), Quiet Quitting, Goof-off Simulation, Work Life Balance (Steam). All small, solo, action/stealth. **Idea exists; nobody made it strategic or social.**

### Seed idea 2: manager has to lay someone off

Tiltfactor **LAYOFF** (2009, match-3 firing, ~1.1M players week 1 *claimed*), Resume Please! (iOS), Work to Death, Enterprise Employer, Big Chief (Reigns-style swipe to fire). **Idea exists; always solo, always the player alone vs the system.**

### Multiplayer office (the thin, proven slice)

| Game | Facts | Weakness we exploit |
|---|---|---|
| Dale & Dawson Stationery Supplies (Aug 2024, $7.99) | ~226K copies, ~$1.5M gross *est.*; **91% of ~9K reviews**; 6.4K peak; now dozens to low hundreds online | Real-time voice social deduction, needs 6+ at once, players drift away |
| Deducto 1/2 | Office hidden-role party game, 6-16 players | Same: real-time, voice, big groups |
| Risk: Office Politics, Office Life (board games) | Tabletop | Not digital |

### Adjacent references (mechanics to learn from)

| Game | Lesson |
|---|---|
| Suzerain (93% of 11K reviews) | Political choices with stakeholders sell; text-heavy is fine |
| Reigns (18M+ claimed) | Tiny UI, strong hook, binary choices |
| Despotism 3k, Papers Please | People-as-resources satire works |
| Blood on the Clocktower, Secret Hitler, Diplomacy | Bluffing, hidden goals, non-binding deals drive replay |
| LinkedIn Games | Daily + colleague leaderboards: **84% next-day return** *claimed* |

## Market signals

| Signal | So what |
|---|---|
| Premium ($5-20) is still the default model for small indie teams; F2P needs live ops | Charge once. Keep it simple. |
| Party/social games price low ($5-10) and grow by friends inviting friends | Only the host should have to pay |
| LinkedIn moved from company to connection leaderboards (Jul 2025), shipped 8 games in ~2 years | Daily + "beat my coworkers" is a habit |
| 2026 tech layoffs: roughly 1 in 5 blamed on AI; RTO friction | Satire is timely and relatable |
| Web portals (Poki 100M MAU) are 99% hypercasual/puzzle playtime | Browser is right, portals are not our channel; our channel is invite links |
| Discord Activities: huge reach, no browsable store | Integration later, not a launch platform |
| Corporate game-based learning (US) $1.5B (2024) to $4.5B (2034) *est.* | Possible B2B team-building plan later |

## Risks seen in the market

- **Player-count decay** in multiplayer games (Dale & Dawson). Mitigation: async mode, leagues, Daily solo mode.
- **Satire fatigue**: the theme is everywhere. Mitigation: the satire is in the rules (politics as a system), not just jokes.
- **Name**: "Reorg" was the former brand of Octus (financial data company). Check trademark before going public.
